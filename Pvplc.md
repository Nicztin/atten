Welcome to NE ATTEN Jewelry and Watch! This repository outlines the details regarding the privacy policy, data handling practices, and intellectual property related to our website and services.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01102015

At NE ATTEN Jewelry and Watch, your privacy is important to us. We are committed to protecting your personal information. Below is an overview of how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, billing/shipping address, and payment details.

Non-Personal Information: IP address, browser type, device details, cookies.


## How We Use Your Information

Order processing and fulfillment.

Marketing communications (only for opted-in users).

Website improvements and user experience enhancement.

Customer support and response to inquiries.


Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share your information with trusted third parties such as payment processors, shipping providers, and email service providers.

These third parties are required to protect your data confidentiality.


Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your user experience and provide tailored content. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.


Data Protection

We implement secure encryption, servers, and access protocols to safeguard your personal data.


Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion, and opt-out options for marketing communications are available to you.


## International Transfers

We ensure appropriate safeguards for data transferred outside your country.


Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Effective Date."


## Information for Minors

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
